# Plat Analysis: Section G and Section H

## Physical Layout from Plat Drawing (June 1989)

### Overall Structure
- The plat shows **Section G** (bottom strip, between Gardenia and Heather roads)
- And **Section H** (top two strips, between Heather and Hydrangia roads)
- Roads run **east-west** across the cemetery
- Fodale Avenue runs along the **west** side
- A **12' Drive** separates the upper and lower halves of Section H

### Section G (Bottom Strip - between Gardenia and Heather)
- Single strip of plots running east-west
- Plot widths: **5'** (narrow plots, many per row)
- Rows numbered from west (Fodale) to east
- Plot numbers visible: 1-137+ (far right shows 877, 374, 374 area)
- The section has approximately **137+ rows** running east-west
- Each "block" on the plat contains multiple plots side by side

### Section H (Two Strips - between Heather and Hydrangia)
- **TWO strips** separated by a 12' Drive in the middle
- Upper strip (north of 12' Drive): rows numbered from east to west
- Lower strip (south of 12' Drive): rows numbered from west to east
- Plot widths: **9'** (wider plots)
- Plot numbers visible: 1-382 (lower strip), then continuing in upper strip

### Key Pattern Observations from Image

**Section G pattern** (from plat):
- Blocks of 1-4 plots wide
- Pattern repeats: narrow blocks (1 plot) then widening (2, 3, 4 plots)
- Total rows: ~386 (from seed data)
- The blocks correspond to physical burial lots

**Section H pattern** (from plat):
- Blocks of 1-4 plots wide  
- Has a 12' Drive dividing it into two halves
- Upper half: rows 75+ (higher numbers)
- Lower half: rows 1-74
- Total rows: ~582 (from seed data)
