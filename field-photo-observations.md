## Key Observations

### Street Layout (West to East)
The roads run **north-south** as lanes, and the sections are bounded between them:
- **Gardenia Ln** — western boundary (Section F/G boundary)
- **Gladiola Ln** — appears to be a cross-lane WITHIN Section G (not in the original plat names)
- **Heather Ln** — boundary between Section G and Section H
- **Hydrangia Ln** — eastern boundary of Section H

### Important Discovery: GLADIOLA Ln
The plat uses "Gardenia" and "Heather" as the G section boundaries, but the field photos show a **GLADIOLA Ln** sign. This may be:
1. A cross-lane running perpendicular (east-west) through the G section
2. OR the actual name of what the plat calls "Gardenia" (spelling/naming discrepancy)

Looking at the photos more carefully:
- IMG_9068 shows Gardenia Ln at the END of the road (the road terminates there)
- IMG_9076 shows Gladiola Ln at an INTERSECTION (cross-street)
- This confirms Gladiola Ln is a **cross-lane** running east-west through the cemetery

### Plot Layout Observations
- The plots near **Gardenia Ln** (IMG_9068) show the typical 5' wide G-section pattern: narrow plots in groups
- The plots near **Hydrangia Ln** (IMG_9072, IMG_9075) show wider 9' plots consistent with Section H
- There is a **large open area** between Heather Ln and Hydrangia Ln — this is the Section H expansion area
- The **DANIEL** plot is a large double-wide plot (granite border) consistent with a 9' or 13.5' H-section plot
