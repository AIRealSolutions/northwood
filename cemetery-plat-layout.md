# Northwood Cemetery - Official Plat Layout

## Overview
Based on official survey drawings by Tide Water Engineering and Surveying P.A.
City of Southport, Smithville Township, Brunswick County, NC
Date: June 1989

## Cemetery Boundaries
- **West**: Fodale Avenue
- **North**: Cape Fear River Heights - Section 2
- **East**: Section H boundary
- **South**: Row 1

## Sections A-F (West Side)
- Located on the west side of the cemetery
- Each section is 2 lots wide
- Rows numbered 1-74 (south to north)
- Lot positions: odd numbers on west side, even numbers on east side of each section

### Section Layout (West to East):
| Section | West Lots | East Lots | Width |
|---------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| A | 37 | 38 | 90' |
| B | 39 | 40 | 90' |
| C | 37 | 38 | 100' |
| D | 37 | 38 | 100' |
| E | 37 | 38 | 79' |
| F | 37 | 38 | - |

### Row Numbering (Sections A-F):
- Row 1 at south end
- Row 74 at north end (near Fodale Ave)
- Total: 74 rows

## Sections G & H (East Side)
- Located on the east side of the cemetery
- Much larger sections with internal 12' drives
- Three internal drives: Hydrangia, Heather, Gardinia

### Section G:
- West boundary: adjacent to Section F
- Contains lots numbered in sequence
- Internal drives divide the section

### Section H:
- Easternmost section
- Largest section
- Lot numbers up to 877+
- Internal 12' drives: Hydrangia Drive, Heather Drive, Gardinia Drive

## Internal Drives (12' wide)
1. **Hydrangia Drive** - runs east-west through upper portion of G & H
2. **Heather Drive** - runs east-west through middle of G & H
3. **Gardinia Drive** - runs east-west through lower portion of G & H

## Plot Numbering Convention
- Format: NW-[Section]-[Row]-[Position]
- Example: NW-A-15-1 = Section A, Row 15, Position 1 (west side)
- Example: NW-H-234 = Section H, Lot 234
